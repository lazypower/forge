# Forge code of conduct

Forge is a personal project, but its public collaboration spaces should remain
safe and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss the work rather than attacking the person doing it.
- Interpret mistakes in good faith while remaining clear about their effects.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept correction, de-escalate conflict, and stop an interaction when asked.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Forge repositories and other spaces where someone is
representing the project. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions,
restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the
project and its community.

To report conduct privately, use the confidential process described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the request as a conduct report. If no
private channel is available, request one without publishing names, allegations,
screenshots, or other sensitive details.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Forge cannot promise a
particular response time or outcome.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 1.4 and the code of conduct
inherited with the Gitea source. It has been shortened to describe Forge's
actual governance and reporting capacity.
